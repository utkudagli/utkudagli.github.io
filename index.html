Projects

Heretic

2024

PC — Unreal Engine 5

Description

Rogue-like fantasy game set in Medieval Europe. As a mercenary, you need to find and defeat the witch who put a curse on you to get rid of the Inquisition after you. This game was designed and developed for my master's thesis, "Punishment Mechanics in Video Games: Effects on Player Experience and Flow", exploring how players react to punishment mechanics and what motivates them to continue playing despite challenges.

My Role

  • Designed the complete game systems, level layouts, AI behaviours and attack patterns
  • Developed and implemented all game mechanics
  • Balanced mechanics and stats throughout development
  • Led both design and development as solo developer

About

Player Character

Class: Designer / Developer

Utku Dağlı is a Designer specialised in Game, Technical and UI/UX Design. He has been designing games for the last two years and has developed games for over seven years. He has worked on several projects, from computer games to VR/AR, including one published game on Steam and several VR/AR projects.

He has experience with Unreal Engine 4 and 5 for over seven years and Unity for over four years. His engineering degree improved his skills in analysing and problem-solving, while his master's program deepened his perspectives on games and inspired him to study user experience.

He worked as a Tutor at Universität Klagenfurt, helping and guiding students to discuss and analyse games under the Klagenfurt Game Lab. He has been a member of the Klagenfurt Game Jam Organisation Team for the last five game jams.
